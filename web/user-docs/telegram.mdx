---
title: "Telegram"
description: "Message it from your phone. The assistant still runs on your computer."
---

1. In Telegram, open @BotFather and send `/newbot`. Copy the token.
2. In `~/.paperclip/.env`, set `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` to that token.
3. Set `TELEGRAM_ALLOWED_USER` to your numeric Telegram id (from @userinfobot). If you leave this empty, anyone who finds the bot can talk to your assistant.

```bash
paperclip telegram
```

Leave that process running. Your computer calls Telegram, so you do not need a public address.

Only plain messages are answered. Bot commands are ignored. If the allowlist is set, other people get a short refusal.

The computer prints your message and the reply. You read the reply in Telegram.
